# Dimension change, version 2

## Frozen inputs and actual upstream scope

Use the frozen periods and dimension-change-definition/v2 input. The definition contains the expected group, two periods, ordered dimensions, exact filters and this version's native-parsed method pin. It contains no scopeRef. Both selectedEntities and selectionEvidence must bind to the same directly dependent upstream step's named STEP_OUTPUT ports.

An unfinished producer is an ordinary dependency, not an empty selection or an authorization failure. Do not look up fabricated artifact IDs, insert a placeholder ScopeRef or change the frozen Plan while waiting. When the producer succeeds, resolve the two actual artifact IDs from its durable outputs, and verify that they are the real sealed selection pair.

Read the original ScopeArtifact referenced by that pair under current authorization. Its actual metadata and payload determine the original scopeRef. Verify owner, source hashes, scope, group, both actual date windows and period references against the frozen definition. Check the approved dimension-change version and native content digest on execution and recovery. The method document is not an authority grant.

Publish the genuine SelectedScopeArtifact through the registered LOCAL calculation boundary. Its members are exactly the selected entities, sorted by link ID and hashed over the complete set. The inherited enumeration version describes the original source; it is neither a fresh authorization check nor an assertion that this subset is the full group. Do not manufacture ScopeArtifact, GroupMembersPage or authority responses for selected members.

## Real joint queries and continuation

Use the ordered joint dimensions province and device. Apply the frozen IN or IS_UNKNOWN filters exactly, without guessing geographic aliases, device names or additional conditions. Each cohort contains at most 500 selected members. Query the same frozen cohort and filters for both periods through real asynchronous DIMENSION_BREAKDOWN requests at the frozen selected-member endpoint.

Keep one dimension_change SKILL step and its actual child ledger. Do not wrap invented TOOL Plan steps. Logical child slots are stable across retries, while request hashes bind the actual executor version, original frozen definition and derived scope. Recheck the real selection pair, original scope, derived shard and current authorization before each wire operation. A changed request must conflict with its original child, not acquire a new identity.

Receive and verify every result page. A top list, missing page or explicit TOO_LARGE response cannot become a complete joint distribution. Preserve original job identities during waiting, recovery, capacity deferral and release. Reuse READY evidence and completed LOCAL publications without resubmitting jobs or recalculating completed pages.

## Numbers and evidence limits

The analysis unit is SHARD_COHORT. Each comparison describes one exact shard cohort across two periods; it is not a per-link distribution, a full-group result or an independent whole-selection total. Use actual province-by-device buckets rather than combining separate marginal rankings.

Preserve KNOWN values exactly and keep UNKNOWN distinct from NOT_APPLICABLE. A missing counterpart bucket can represent zero observed matching clicks only after the complete query, source scope, filters and bucket totals are verified. This does not prove complete telemetry or zero real visits.

For each period, bucket PV must sum to its independent summary PV and ratioDenominator. Shares use that period's complete filtered window for that cohort. Preserve both denominators when comparing shares. A zero baseline has no relative percentage change.

Use each query's independently deduplicated whole-window UV and UIP, retaining approximation metadata. Never sum UV or UIP across buckets, links, days or shards, and never derive a whole-selection distinct count by adding shard summaries.

Conclusions remain OBSERVED_ONLY. Retain collection-quality UNKNOWN, missing metrics, dimension coverage, approximation and comparability limits. Different query snapshots do not establish a common historical snapshot. Structural completeness does not establish causality, object lifetime or complete event collection.

## Empty selections and publication

For a published empty selection with NO_DECLINES, publish SelectedScope and then dimensionChanges with evidenceDisposition NOT_APPLICABLE. Submit no asynchronous statistics requests and make no claim that dimensions did not change.

For an empty selection with INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, also submit no statistics requests and preserve that limitation. A nonempty partial selection describes only its explicit members and retains selectionComplete and its source limitations.

Publish bounded LOCAL comparison pages and a final manifest referencing the complete chain, SelectedScope and original evidence. Output expiry must not exceed any input. Keep bulk rows outside Graph state. A successful Skill or structurally complete artifact does not mark the user goal ANSWERED and does not bypass common report assembly or goal assessment.
